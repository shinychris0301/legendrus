# v1.19 Step 3 — Post-Launch Trend, Retention, and Economy Guardrail Review

This checkpoint adds local aggregate evidence review after launch. Guardrails are project-specific and must be explicitly retained/reviewed before evidence can be classified.

The evaluator reviews active-user/session/completion trends, D1/D7/D30 cohort retention, soft-currency source/sink balance, hard-currency net grants, booster net grants, and aggregate reward-unit movement. Every recommendation is informational and requires human review.

No Step 3 code mutates economy catalogs, balances, inventory, rewards, levels, tutorials, saves, remote config, monetization, or Play Console state. No player identifiers or raw event streams are persisted by this foundation.
