# Monetization & Retention Implementation Notes

The story documents support:
- Living Light energy as the session gate
- boosters justified as story items
- chapter-finale offers
- character rescue events
- cosmetic progression tied to chapters/achievements
- seasonal overlays/events
- rewarded-video opportunities
- fail/continue purchases

The current vertical slice implements only non-charging development mocks.

## Commercial principles
- purchases must be explicit and reversible where platform policy requires
- local pricing comes from the store, not baked image text
- no fake scarcity or misleading timers
- rewarded ads are optional and accurately described
- spending should accelerate/convenience/cosmeticize, not invalidate normal progression
- story and attachment remain valuable without purchases

## Metrics to instrument before tuning
- tutorial completion
- first-level completion/fail
- level retry rate
- energy depletion
- shop open rate
- purchase funnel
- rewarded-ad opt-in
- chapter milestone retention
- character/story interaction rate
- D1/D7/D30 retention
