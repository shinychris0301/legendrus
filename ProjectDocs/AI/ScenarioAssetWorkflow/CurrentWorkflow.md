## Workflow to apply to further art work

1. Inspect the actual reference and current Unity capture. Inventory reusable approved assets and classify each discrepancy as layout/text, clean source extraction, bounded repair, or regeneration. Record why a source component cannot be recovered before choosing regeneration.
2. For recoverable icons, buttons, plaques and frames, use the established Scenario isolation workflow, retaining original RGB wherever possible. Guidance crops are staging inputs; scenery-contaminated rectangles must not enter runtime. Preserve intentionally opaque panel faces.
3. Apply the returned alpha locally, with the user's authorized cleanup as needed. Record repaired regions and limitations. Do not describe painted-over or hidden pixels as exactly recovered. Regenerate only the inaccessible/unsuitable portions, such as obscured backgrounds or difficult character edges, when the asset assessment supports it.
4. Handle live/localizable labels and changing balances, dates and quantities in Unity. Previously approved source-lettering treatments are page-specific; avoid treating a baked label as live localization. Font identity remains unknown unless actually established.
5. Inspect isolated assets over black, white and saturated backgrounds and at intended phone display size. Verify source colors, edge cleanliness, opaque surfaces and aspect ratio. Preserve original/generation outputs and source-coordinate provenance.
6. Build the actual owning prefab/runtime together, preserving GUIDs and unrelated pages. Review fresh Unity captures against the original; targeted behavior/layout checks supplement visual review but do not prove exact fidelity.
7. Record the complete non-secret generation parameters, source hash/bounds, model, existing job ID, status, cost, output hash, local processing and acceptance/rejection reason. Poll submitted jobs instead of issuing duplicate paid requests. Any further generation should address a specific documented discrepancy.

For Events, the next art-focused assessment should examine the original title/card/tab ornamentation for clean extraction and identify precisely which painting regions need repair.
