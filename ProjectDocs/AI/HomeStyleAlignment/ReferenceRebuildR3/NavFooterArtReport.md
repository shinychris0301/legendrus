# Home R3 navigation and footer asset generation

Six individual control PNGs are saved in `Sources/`: `nav_map_generated.png`, `nav_shop_generated.png`, `nav_events_generated.png`, `nav_characters_generated.png`, `mail_medallion_generated.png`, and `reward_medallion_generated.png`. All are 1254 × 1254 RGBA and unchanged copies of selected built-in imagegen output bytes. Exact prompts, original generator paths, selected project paths and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `NavFooterPrompts.json`.

The original whole `Assets/LegendsOfRus/Art/ReferenceOnly/ApprovedScreens/Home.png` and the previous actual Unity Home R2 portrait were directly inspected. R2 mismatches included thin rounded bright frames, simplified medallions without surrounding carved wood, an open blue helmet and a hanging cloth banner rather than the reference's closed bronze noseguard helmet and red parchment scroll. New generation restores those subject forms, heavy clipped-corner bronze frames, curling botanical filigree, sapphire crests and darker controlled metal color.

Each selected output was independently generated from scratch with a textual description informed by the full original reference. No source screenshot pixels were extracted, cropped, traced or used as runtime art. No PNG pixel edits, recoloring, alpha removal, resize or compositing were applied after generation. An initial whole-reference generation produced RGB with a baked checkerboard and was rejected; a second map candidate was rejected for geometric knotwork/compass disk mismatch. Selected fresh generations have real alpha.

Read-only Chrome review displays all six selected files over black, white and saturated magenta. Captures: `QA/NavFooterReview_panels.png` and `QA/NavFooterReview_small.png`; browser source and exact alpha histograms: `QA/NavFooterReview.html`, `QA/CaptureNavFooterReview.mjs`, `QA/NavFooterAlphaChecks.json`. Direct inspection found no visible checkerboard, scenery fragments, rectangle halos or baked labels. Panel surfaces are near opaque alpha252–253, so opaque native navy backings remain appropriate. Source paths/hashes were checked after copying. Chrome required an automatically approved sandbox escalation after local startup timed out.

This report records asset generation and browser diagnostics, not Unity or device verification. These are newly rendered interpretations and exact pixel or color identity to the reference is not established. Navigation cards leave blank live-text strips; the builder must place each live caption inside its actual strip and account for differing transparent margins.
