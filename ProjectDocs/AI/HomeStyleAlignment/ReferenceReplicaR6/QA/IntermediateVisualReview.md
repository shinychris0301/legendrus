2. **The reward inset is too tall and extends too far down.** The current inset ends around y1643, below the main quest panel's bottom rail. The original inset appears closer to128–135px high. Recheck its intended visible rectangle and move/resize the whole framed resource before revisiting transparent margins. Keep the gem and reward value aligned independently.

3. **The footer starts with a hard black strip.** At the941px composition, the independent footer's black upper area creates a straight boundary around y1450 behind the mail/gift and above the wood panel. The reference carries dark environmental/ornamental texture through this transition. The all-black flat strip is particularly noticeable against the brighter current cloak and tail just above it. Consider how much of the intentionally opaque footer plate must start this high; placement or a properly reconstructed upper edge may reduce the seam without using the reference as runtime UI.

4. **The top resource gem is vertically flattened relative to the reference.** Its current diamond is broad and shallow beside the balance, while the reference has a longer lower point and taller silhouette. This is separate from the already handled maximum-balance wrapping. Review the glyph-free icon's complete-source transform and reference visible bounds; the current card-frame/icon geometry should not determine its aspect. - Ivan's head placement is near the reference, but the new painting shows a more upright full body, hands and a detailed visible waist/belt beneath the Start region. The reference composition presents a larger torso with its lower body concealed by the controls. The current brighter royal-blue clothing, gold embroidery and broad red cloak also differ from the reference's darker navy treatment. A lower fade fixes the straight cutoff but does not make the pose or clothing identical.
- The Firebird's main perch is recognizable, but its lower tail forms a denser, brighter orange mass and uses different feather paths and jewels. The original has more separated fine strands and different negative spaces. A whole combined Ivan/Firebird transform cannot independently reproduce those details.
- The crest's silhouette, Cyrillic lettering, filigree and highlights remain repainted variants. Its broad position is close; scaling alone will not make the interior letterforms and scrollwork exact.
- Navigation now has coherent card silhouettes and main illustration types, but the frame ornaments are more uniform and brighter than the source. Map markings, shop goods, scroll motif and helmet engraving differ. The frame reference does not have every same top ornament on all four cards.

Because the envelope and disk are a combined sprite, reducing the whole medallion also shrinks the correctly placed outer rim; exact internal proportions require a refined isolated resource or separate icon layering. The gift's overall chest/medallion relation is closer, with different fittings and jewels.
