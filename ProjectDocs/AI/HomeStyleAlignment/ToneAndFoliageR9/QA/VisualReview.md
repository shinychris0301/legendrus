# R9 final visual review

Reviewed the actual final Unity 941×1672 Russian and English, 576×1280 simulated safe-area and 1080×2400 simulated safe-area Home captures. An independent reviewer compared final Russian/reference-size and narrow-phone portraits with their accepted R8 counterparts and the complete original design reference. Map detail was also checked through the standalone viewer.

The subdued olive/bronze leaves now appear beside Map instead of being hidden by Ivan's cloak. The final smaller/lower placement tucks them behind the frame; the card face, label and interactive area remain clear. At narrow phone scale the cluster remains visible and follows the navigation group. No obvious background rectangle or edge fringe appeared in runtime views or in the full isolated sprite's black/white/magenta compositing review.

Lower stones and edge foliage are quieter. Shading remains beneath the separate hero and controls, with no new hard edge or bottom seam in the reviewed portrait sizes. Sunset, crest, opaque hero/Firebird regions, accepted Play lettering and both badges retain their appearance. Read-only pixel comparisons establish zero change in the upper Home region and exact RU roundtrip/Splash/Loading reference matches; Play Mode toggle tests separately verify opaque artwork protection.

The generated sprig is a close stylistic approximation, not the reference's identical leaf arrangement. Existing gold/blue assets remain brighter and more uniformly highlighted than the original reference. That separate material issue is still visible; this pass does not assert complete reference identity. Accepted illustration differences, mailbox proportion and unavailable live XP/quest/event-state gaps are unchanged.

The first visual iteration placed the upper leaf slightly high and large. A 10% reduction and 15px downward move improved the fit. The first portrait also caught unfinished button color transitions; the corrected fixture waits for their real completion. Only final passing captures are exposed in the review viewer; failed iteration evidence is retained separately.

No further placement or shading correction is recommended for this focused pass. User acceptance of R9 is not inferred. Physical-phone appearance and performance have not been tested.
