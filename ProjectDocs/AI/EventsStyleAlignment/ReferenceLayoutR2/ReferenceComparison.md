The last card is approximately13px shorter than the original to retain a visible Back control. No reward pictures or amounts are presented as promised prizes because there are no authoritative event reward recipes. The reference's three illustrative prizes per card, premium ribbon and active timers remain absent intentionally.

Remaining visual differences include exact plaque/rail engraving, tab shapes/texture, font identity, title glyph embossing, character poses and scenery. R2 reuses the paintings; it does not claim to recreate the original characters or environment. The shared player header retains its accepted portrait and real balances; no fabricated XP fraction was added. EventDetail remains its existing demo implementation.
