Compared the supplied original to fresh Unity captures. R3 repairs the specific extraction/layout defects identified in the [R2 fidelity audit](../ReferenceLayoutR2/QA/ReferenceFidelityReview.md).

| Area | R3 result | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Title face | Dark old letter silhouettes removed; live heading preserved | Donor texture and current serif/bevel are not the exact original lettering/material |
| Panel contours | Connected gold/dark-metal edges and source corners visible again; no color-threshold holes | Repaired blank inner faces and small edge details remain approximate |
| Lower portraits | Complete curved lower rims and beard/neck caps; no horizontal cutoffs | Only lower caps use Scenario reconstruction; hidden original pixels cannot be recovered exactly |
| Row artwork | Portraits and rewards keep source pixel proportions on a foreground layer | Low source resolution limits detail; natural overlaps remain intentional |
| Power and separators | Common right edge, centered gap dots clear of row rails | Native font metrics differ from the reference |
| Rules overlay | Repaired corners and unstretched repeating material; original behavior retained | This is native explanatory UI, not a separate original-reference replica |

Medal depth, finer typography and the carved full footer remain candidates for the next pass. Header/scenery differences and truthful preview/live-state differences remain documented in R2. R3 does not add real ranking/season/reward functionality.
