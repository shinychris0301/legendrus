# Chapter 1: all 500 rounds

Normal Home → Chapter Map uses the installed chapter. Existing progress is retained; a completed round-200 save can select 201. The finale is at 500. Chapter 2 is still shown as unavailable; Chapter Complete returns to the World Map.

For recorded testing without changing normal progress:

1. In Unity 6000.5.9f1 enter Play Mode and reach Home with no modal open.
2. Open **Legends of Rus → Development → Campaign Active Playtest (Recorded)**.
3. Choose any installed ten-round block, from **1–10** through **491–500**, then start the recording. Test 201–210 first and include the harder rounds and recovery rounds. Existing 31–200 difficulty remains unverified too.
4. The disposable fixture starts with 30 energy and zero inventory boosters. Earlier completions are setup data, not playtest results. Try without boosters first; record failures/retries as well as wins.
5. Before Continue/Retry, rate difficulty, goal clarity and special-item opportunity. Finish the block's dialogue, then save and exit from the playtest completion page. The development recording ends at its selected block; normal-game Chapter Complete navigation is a separate integration check.
6. Use Show recording / Copy recording path. Exports are stored under `Application.persistentDataPath/CampaignPlaytests/Sessions/manual_campaign/`, with a fingerprint of the installed content. No upload occurs.

Assess whether the six late sectors feel distinct, how often goals require planning, whether recovery rounds relieve pressure, and whether 500 feels like a conclusion. Solver solutions are not player difficulty measurements. Older candidate menu entries refer to historical content; use **Active** for this chapter.

An interrupted recording is retained as interrupted; it does not resume its board. Same-attempt continuation is still a separate deferred feature. No animation/effect redesign is part of this delivery.
