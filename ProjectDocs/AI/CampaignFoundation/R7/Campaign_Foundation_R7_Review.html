What should change first

  1. Round design: clear teaching/practice/mastery/recovery roles, open and constrained paths, reachable targets and useful opportunities to create specials.
  2. On-board power-ups: distinct directional arrow, seeking Firebird Spark, Thunder Stone blast and Living Light. Add tap activation and test combinations.
  3. Obstacles: visibly cracked ice, snapped chains, lost crate planks; then one new power-up-only runic seal.
  4. Effects: charge → travel/impact → material-specific debris → settle, with distinct behavior for each existing inventory booster.

Keep approved gems, UI, Russian-fairy-tale materials and inventory ownership. New sprites need an asset review. No purchased item changes meaning silently. The next playable slice should prove the mechanics before 40 more rounds are authored.
