What should change

Mass before light

Uneven, thick, texture-bearing chunks and short grounded shadows make jewels feel solid. A compact glint supports the fracture.

A different silhouette per power

A line item travels. A bomb expands violently. A Firebird lifts, banks and strikes. A rainbow gathers and selects before releasing.

Controlled escalation

Three-match → special → combination. Reserve the widest debris, strongest recoil and longest staging for premium combinations.

Full effect direction

FamilyProposed choreography
Ordinary gemsBrief compression → asymmetric fracture → thick foreground chunks and small rear chips → fast fade. Keep the gem's original color and facets.
Line / columnItem locks into its direction → two forceful moving heads with tapered exhaust → each cell fractures at contact → wake clears quickly. Rotate the whole motion for columns.
BombCompression and a small lift → shaped hot core and expanding smoke lobes → shell and jewel debris → short board-local recoil and a clean recovery.
Creation / rainbowPieces gather into a clearly forming object. Rainbow targets acknowledge selection before discharge; use sparse, staggered connections and visible transformation.
Firebird / carried itemsArticulated wing poses, lift shadow, banked flight and deliberate landing. Payload stays visible and fires only on arrival.
Mace / Living Light / Sea PearlMace: heavy swing and rebound. Living Light: shield-like facet unfolds and sends its existing footprint outward. Sea Pearl: water lens gathers pieces, rotates and releases them into the existing shuffle.
ObstaclesWood planks split; ice sheds translucent plates; chain links tension and snap; seal face fractures. First hit retains damaged artwork. Final hit breaks the remaining structure.
CombinationsBrief visible fusion → recognizable combined attack → sequential contact and debris. Give every supported pair its own staging, derived from its actual affected cells.
