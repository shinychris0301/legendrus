Gameplay R24 · Actual Unity review

Clearer effects without circular overlays.

The generic creation halo and circular impact glows have been removed. Accent brightness now stays continuous at the reveal, opposed ribbons no longer form a loop, and effects retain their shape when crossing the board edge.

What caused the glitches

  • The shared pooled presenter continued emitting SpecialCreationHalo and cascade/booster pulses alongside the new item renderers. R4 now retains only its non-circular swap trail.
  • The older impact renderer still listened to creation events and added another generic glow beneath the new artwork.
  • Ordinary gem contacts left circular additive light spots that outlasted the immediate impact.
  • Mirrored and orbiting accents could close into a visible loop.
  • Accent alpha jumped from 0.5 to 1.0 at the start of completion.
  • Clamping individual mesh vertices squashed off-board effects into the border.
  • Six rotated copies of the bomb blast produced a repeated stamp pattern. They have been removed.
