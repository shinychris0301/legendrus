Gameplay R25 · Actual Unity review

Fine chips, larger shards and sparkling impact.

Each shattered gem releases eight irregular material shards and sixteen tiny chips. Short colored glints travel with the chips, with a tapered flare at contact. Line items trigger each burst as the moving front reaches that gem.

Shatter choreography

  • At contact: a brief colored flare and a white-hot faceted glint; no expanding ring.
  • First 0.13 seconds: large irregular shards separate while tiny chips accelerate outwards.
  • 0.13–0.55 seconds: small particles spread in varied directions, trailing light and fading at different times.
  • Up to 0.62 seconds: larger pieces tumble and fall, retaining the original gem texture.
  • Row and column clears use the existing cell-contact schedule, so the bursts follow the travelling item.

Normal-speed playback shows the final rhythm. Half speed and the frame slider expose the material and light layers.
