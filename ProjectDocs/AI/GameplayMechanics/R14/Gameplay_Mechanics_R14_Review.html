Distinct materials, clearer impacts

Wood, ice, chains and seals now break differently. Gems retain their approved textures while shattering into asymmetric facets.

Implemented

  • Wood releases grain splinters and larger jagged planks on its final hit.
  • Ice cracks into translucent lit facets.
  • Antique-gold chains vibrate on damage and scatter open links on release.
  • Seals shed gold and sapphire rune fragments.
  • Ordinary gems flash briefly and break into varied textured facets. Specials retain their own effects.
