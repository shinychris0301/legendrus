Record a manual session
  1. Enter Play Mode and reach Home. Choose Legends of Rus → Development → Gameplay Mechanics Sandbox.
  2. Play a round without reading the R4 solution. Tap Difficulty, Goals and Effects to cycle optional ratings. Leave them unrated when you have no opinion.
  3. Use Retry or Next to continue. Wins, losses and interrupted attempts are kept separately; an animation interrupted by navigation is marked unsettled.
  4. Press Export session JSON before Exit or stopping Play Mode. Copy location gives the latest file path. Files are stored under Application.persistentDataPath/MechanicsPlaytests. Nothing is uploaded.

Records are in memory until explicit export. Each export is a new snapshot; export again after more play or feedback. A currently running attempt stays ongoing in that file. Leaving the sandbox discards unexported memory. The last200 closed attempts plus one active attempt are retained; discarded counts are reported.
