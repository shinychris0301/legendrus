# Profile Reference Rebuild R1 — reference fidelity

Reference: `Sources/Reference.png`, copied from `/home/k2/Downloads/match3final/profile.png` (941 × 1672). Implemented views are actual Unity captures in `UnityCaptures/`, not offline mockups.

The page follows the reference's portrait/identity/stat hierarchy, four adjacent tabs, five hero cards, six relic cards and three footer actions. This is not a pixel-exact reproduction.

## Improved in this pass

- Actual shared `ProductionPlayerHeader` component, including coins, gems and energy; the Home family resource header replaces the earlier individual cloned controls.
- Original Profile title and portrait reused; wood backing spans the title area.
- Original hanging banner retained in a continuous identity panel. Two continuous framed stat columns replace six separately framed rows.
- Source-derived tab faces and carved collection/details surfaces. Old labels under dynamic content are cleared with recorded source texture donors.
- Wooden footer surfaces use original source carving with existing Profile, trophy and history icons. Back and footer actions sit near the safe bottom edge; the shared header sits at the safe top edge.
- Dark content backing keeps Items/History headings readable over the shared Home scenery.

## Intentional differences approved earlier

- Saved best score, cleared levels, attempts, stars, chapter and unlocked level replace untracked combat statistics.
- The identity bar reports actual Chapter 1 completion, not invented hero XP. Header balances and level use the save, not reference example numbers.
- Hero/relic panels say “design preview”; level circles show dashes and no collection ownership/upgrade system is implied. Source stars are decorative preview artwork.
- The reference's upgrade action is replaced with an explanation/action panel. It does not grant items or perform unsupported upgrades.
- Approved existing hero portraits, relic artwork, shared boosters and Home header are reused.

## Remaining visual differences

1. Live text uses approved local fonts; it is heavier and wider than the reference in several names, stat labels and captions. Exact source font identity remains unverified.
2. The relic collection's continuous enclosing carving, heading ornaments and card-caption geometry need another pass. Some captions wrap where the original uses small single-line text.
3. Hidden navy/wood material was reconstructed from clean source donors. Repetition and rounded boundaries do not exactly reproduce the missing source pixels.
4. Three individual footer faces approximate the reference's continuous footer strip; selection glow and internal separators differ.
5. The detail panel deliberately shows preview information. On tall screens it expands between content and footer, leaving more empty interior space than the reference.
6. Home scenery and a native dark backing replace the original full carved backdrop. Existing modal and secondary-tab panel materials remain approximate.

Recommended R2: relic collection frame, heading ornaments and caption fit, preserving the shared assets and real-stat contract.
