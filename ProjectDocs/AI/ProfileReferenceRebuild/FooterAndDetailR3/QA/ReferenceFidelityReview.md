# Profile R3 — footer and selected-relic detail

Compare the actual Unity views with the original Profile reference in the review selector. The original is retained in `../R1/Sources/Reference.png`.

## Improved

- One continuous source-derived wooden strip replaces the three independent curved footer frames. The original divider positions and broader Profile action area are restored.
- All three actions remain separate native buttons. Their face textures align with the underlying strip, and selection tint fades to unchanged edge pixels.
- The default selected sword uses the original larger detail artwork and its wider frame. The final source crop was expanded beyond the initial tight crop to retain the corner curls.
- Selected-relic title, description, preview badge and action occupy the reference's general artwork/text/action arrangement. The badge explicitly says Preview rather than inventing rarity or ownership.
- The approved detail surface now uses sprite borders so its corners do not stretch on tall phones. Content is centered vertically within the extra panel height.
- Header, stats, tabs and collections above y1266 are pixel-identical to R2 in the compared standard RU/EN captures.

## Intentional adaptations

Saved gameplay statistics and clearly labelled collection previews remain as approved earlier. No hero XP, combat attributes, collection ownership, upgrades or economy values are invented. The reference's upgrade controls are replaced by the existing Profile Actions explanation and a working selection hint.

Existing cards are reused for selected relics other than the sword; their aspect ratios and framing differ from the source's larger sword image. The header, Home scenery and approved fonts remain shared with the game.

## Remaining differences

Live lettering is heavier and wider than the reference. Some relic captions remain small to fit without overflow. Hidden wooden/navy faces were reconstructed from source donors, so grain and lighting are approximate. Fine outer alpha contours and the footer crest junction remain approximate. Tall-screen detail panels contain more space than the reference's original aspect ratio.
