# Victory R7 comparison

[Actual Unity portraits, prior R6 and original reference](Victory_FirebirdAndButton_R7_Review.html).

The Firebird now has a smaller/slender body, finer separated wing tips and a longer tail sweeping rightward toward the banner. More scenery shows through its feather gaps; the broad yellow surrounding halo is reduced. Its complete sprite is fitted uniformly into the existing Firebird rectangle, leaving the accepted title/header/reward placement intact. The independent bird is closer in silhouette but still not an exact copy of the reference pose, feather lighting or tail path.

The user's Continue complaint was confirmed: R6 drew a1512×619 sprite into701×164, making the horizontal scale about1.75times its vertical scale. R7 uses new1971×578 art at its native3.41:1 ratio. Runtime width is approximately559.2units at164high, centered, with preserveAspect enabled. The button is intentionally narrower; its gold scrolls and sapphires are no longer flattened. Live RU/EN lettering fits inside the emerald face. This differs from the reference button's exact silhouette but resolves the verified distortion.

R6 message readability, real rewards and native actions remain. Other known differences remain: scene light/architecture, title depth, star rays/confetti, reward silhouettes, footer vine proportions and tall-phone scenery gap. RU story fallback in EN and small energy timer remain. No exact reference fidelity, exact font identity or new user visual acceptance is claimed.
