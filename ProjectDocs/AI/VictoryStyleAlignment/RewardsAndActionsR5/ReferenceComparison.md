# Victory R5 reference comparison

Compared the original approved Victory reference with actual final Unity R5 RU 941×1672 three-reward, EN 576×1280 five-reward, and RU 941×1672 unavailable-video portraits. [Open one portrait at a time](Victory_RewardsAndActions_R5_Review.html).

| Area | R5 result | Remaining difference |
|---|---|---|
| Rewards | Taller cards, larger illustrations and values, stronger gold heading, messages on the enclosing wood surface | Card ornament and existing single coin / wide gem / booster art differ from reference coin stack, tall gem and egg. Actual game rewards are retained. |
| Continue | Wide clipped emerald face, carved gold/sapphire border, closer to reward panel | New independent silhouette and live serif lettering are not pixel-identical. |
| Video action | Rectangular filmstrip play, amber/gold face, coin pile and purse, live gold text | Copy describes the implemented coins-only reward; reference advertises all rewards. Ornament and dimensions differ. |
| Footer | Complete low eagle crest and gold vines; video no longer covers its crown | Gold scrollwork differs; tall-phone layout leaves additional scenery above the crest. |
| Celebration and scene | R4 title/stars and R3 scene/header retained | Prior reference differences remain, including Firebird pose, title contours and confetti density. |

The reward/action group is more coherent and closer to the reference. Exact fidelity is not claimed. The small story/status line near the lower carving and energy timer deserve phone readability review; existing RU story fallback in EN remains visible. Static portraits cannot establish motion quality. The next focused check should be device-size readability and the Victory reveal animation, preserving current artwork and truthful reward behavior.
