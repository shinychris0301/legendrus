# Victory R3 — reference comparison

R3 resolves the largest R2 composition mismatch: a dense illuminated evening city with a floral border replaces the rural village. The Firebird now has separated wing feathers, a slimmer body, a longer flowing tail and transparent golden edge glow. Three substantial wood/gold header compartments replace the thin continuous navy strip; the coin, upright sapphire and cyan lightning materials better follow the reference. Numeric values, refill status and plus controls remain native UI.

Compared original Victory reference with actual R3 Unity Level2 Rewards RU at 941×1672, plus phone-size RU countdown and EN five-reward captures.

| Area | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Background | Much closer architecture, dusk palette and floral framing. Independently generated buildings, sky and flower arrangement still differ. |
| Firebird | Airier wings, longer tail and strong selective glow are closer. Neck, feather patterns, tail trajectory and emission intensity remain independent rather than exact. |
| Header | Three carved compartments, resource silhouettes and real timer inset are now present. Corner motifs, exact proportions and plus ornaments differ; amounts reflect the actual save. |
| Banner/title | R2 treatment retained. Straight live title still differs from the reference's curved, engraved lettering and deeper ribbon. |
| Stars | R2 facets retained; dark seams and limited soft glow still need refinement. |
| Rewards/footer | Native reward grouping retained. Reference icons/cards are larger, framing differs, and footer remains a message strip instead of a large gold crest. |
| Celebration | Reference confetti and surrounding particles remain absent. Current Firebird glow is static sprite artwork, not a new particle system. |

The reference's sample 5,000 / 20 / egg rewards are not game-state requirements; actual currencies/boosters and coins-only video doubling remain truthful.

Recommended next refinement is banner/title and star light treatment, followed by rewards/footer ornamentation. Do not infer an exact match or user acceptance from passing functional tests.
