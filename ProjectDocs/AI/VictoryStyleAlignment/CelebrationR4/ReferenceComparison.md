# Victory R4 — central celebration comparison

The approved next refinement focuses on the ribbon, live title and stars. The R3 scene, Firebird, header, rewards and native actions remain in place.

| Area | R3 | R4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ribbon | Bright crimson, shallow face, large raised leaf motifs and shorter folds. | Darker red-brown face, finer engraved decoration and longer trailing curls. A new isolated blank sprite supplies the artwork. |
| Title | Straight, pale gold lettering. | Live Cyrillic/English glyphs follow a center-high arc with a warmer gold face, bevel and bronze depth. |
| Stars | Sharp dark seams between facets, weaker focal glow. | Layered gold rims and smoother shaded facets, slightly larger/lower group, soft local halos with restrained rays and sparse fixed sparkles. |
| Star state | Earned/unearned state follows current-run result. | Same state and reveal timing; only earned stars show a halo. All halos draw behind every star face. |

The new title follows the reference's arched arrangement more closely. Its serif source, ribbon contour/motifs and native star materials remain independent; this is not a claim of identical artwork or an identified source font. Sparkle details are part of a static mesh, revealed with the existing one-time animation, not a new particle simulation.

Reference differences still outside this pass: reward illustrations/cards are smaller, the gap before Continue is larger (especially on tall phones), Continue/video surfaces differ, and the footer is a story-message strip rather than the reference crest. Those lower-page refinements are the next recommended task. Actual score/rewards, energy capacity and coins-only video doubling must remain faithful to game state.

See [the portrait viewer](Victory_Celebration_R4_Review.html) for individually labelled actual Unity captures, the historical R3 portrait and the original reference. Verification status and limitations are recorded in [ImplementationReport.md](ImplementationReport.md).
