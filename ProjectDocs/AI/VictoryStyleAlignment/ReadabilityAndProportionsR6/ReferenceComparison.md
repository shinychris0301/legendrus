# Victory R6 reference comparison

R6 addresses the approved R5 readability/proportion recommendation. [View actual Unity portraits, R5 and the original reference](Victory_ReadabilityAndProportions_R6_Review.html).

The story/status line now occupies a plain dark inset above the lower carving. It is larger and may wrap onto two lines; the inset disappears when there is no message. Reward labels and grant/replay status are larger. Cards are slightly shorter to give the message and actions room, retaining all actual illustrations and quantities.

Continue and the video action have taller faces and stronger live lettering. Continue remains attached to the reward area, and the video action leaves the footer crest clear. The existing wood/gold/emerald sprites and opaque surfaces are reused without pixel changes.

This is a focused improvement, not an exact reference replica. Firebird pose/tail, scenery, title depth, star light, reward-icon silhouettes and footer vine proportions still differ. The plain message inset carries live content absent from the reference. Tall screens retain scenery space above the bottom crest. Existing RU story fallback in EN and small energy timer remain. Exact source-font identity is unknown; current local fonts remain in use.

Next useful validation: physical-phone readability and the Victory reveal in motion. Static Editor portraits and automated tests cannot establish either conclusively.
