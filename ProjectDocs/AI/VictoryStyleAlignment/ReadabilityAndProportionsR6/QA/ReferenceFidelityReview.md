# Victory R6 — reference fidelity review

Reviewed 2026-09-15 at the user's request. **The material family matches, but the page is still not a faithful visual replica.** R6 improves readability and control proportions; those changes do not resolve the larger artwork differences.

Compared original `Assets/LegendsOfRus/Art/ReferenceOnly/ApprovedScreens/Victory.png` against actual retained R6 Unity portraits `Victory_Level2_Rewards_RU.png` (941×1672, three rewards) and `Victory_Level10_Rewards_576_EN.png` (576×1280, five rewards). [Portrait viewer with original reference](../Victory_ReadabilityAndProportions_R6_Review.html).

| Area | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Materials | Navy evening scene, antique gold, carved wood, emerald Continue and amber video action establish the same family. Ornament patterns and light distribution remain different. |
| Firebird / scene — largest gap | Current bird has a larger upright body, raised symmetrical wings and a compact tail. Reference has a lighter airborne silhouette and a long sweeping tail flowing right/down into the banner. Current orange sunset is more prominent; architecture, sky and floral framing differ. |
| Title / ribbon | Current lettering is thinner, paler and less embossed than the reference's warm orange-gold face. Ribbon curvature, tails and engraving differ. This remains prominent even though the text is live and arched. |
| Stars / celebration | Current faces read as smooth pale gold. Reference has sharper internal facets, stronger rays, sparkles and more surrounding confetti. Static captures cannot verify motion quality. |
| Rewards | Current coin is a single round emblem, gem is a wide diamond and third item is a real booster; reference shows coin stack, upright crystal and egg. Current cards have heavier borders/amount strips and extra label/status text. R6's shorter cards make room for larger message text but are less tall than the reference cards. |
| Story / controls | R6 message is clearly separated from the central lower carving and buttons. Taller actions are an improvement; their lettering, silhouettes and gold shading still differ. The plain message strip is additional functional content absent from the reference. |
| Footer / tall phone | Crest is visible; vines remain lower/flatter than the reference. The tall-phone portrait has a large scenery gap above the crest. Small energy timer and RU story fallback in EN remain visible limitations. |

The original reward amounts, egg and all-rewards video promise are illustrative. Preserve actual inventory/rewards, current score, energy and coins-only doubling; replacing these with reference values would misrepresent the game.

## Next recommendation

Further small spacing changes will not close the dominant gap. If reference fidelity is the next priority, focus the next art revision on **the Firebird silhouette and flowing tail**, using a genuinely isolated sprite and the existing material family. Preserve R6's readability fixes and actual rewards. Title depth and star-light treatment can follow as a separate bounded pass. Physical-phone readability and reveal-motion validation remain useful but were not performed here.
