# R2 — focused reference comparison

Compare one complete portrait at a time in the [review](Gameplay_FramePolish_R2_Review.html): actual R2, previous R1 and original reference. Captures are not retouched.

| Area | R1 gap | R2 result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moves | Clean royal-blue Chapter Map node | Dedicated nearly black lacquer face, gold leaf scrolls, sapphire tips; live text retained |
| Board | Smooth faces and rectangular outer frame | Independent lacquer grain, shaped outer corners, metal joints and sapphire accents |
| Goals | Plain thin rectangle | Material backing, small gold corner ornaments and curls |
| Score | Value directly on a large navy panel | Separate opaque bronze inset, inner shadow and fine gold edging |
| Inactive stars | Filled grey facets | Dark centres with gold outlines, selected by actual score state |

The moves frame's scroll shapes are recreated and its gold is somewhat brighter than the reference. Fine native border ornaments approximate the reference; they are not copied from screenshot regions. The existing environment, portrait, gem/booster colors and layout were preserved. Their previously documented differences remain. The original reference's central flash depicts an action; no flash was added to the idle board. Numeric example values and additional illustrated tile families were not fabricated.

[Implementation, source provenance and exact checks](ImplementationReport.md). R2 implements the first recommended refinement; it does not claim completion of every item in the preceding design audit.
