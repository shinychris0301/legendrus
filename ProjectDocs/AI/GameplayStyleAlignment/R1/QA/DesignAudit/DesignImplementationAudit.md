## Applied artwork and remaining fidelity differences

Five distinct coloured gems and the blue/gold shield are genuinely used for the existing six tile types. The supplied mace, Firebird, shield and potion medallions are also used. Crates, reinforced crates, ice, feathers and four special-piece identities are visible in the retained later-level captures. The separate chain overlay is installed; it avoids placing a baked red gem over every chained tile.

Three runtime atlas PNGs match their supplied originals byte-for-byte. Four crowded regions use the previously documented isolated overrides. This audit did not regenerate, crop, recolor or alter any art. Original-byte equality does not guarantee identical display size, filtering or lighting to the illustrated reference.

The environment follows the dusk/navy/gold family, but its large bright orange sunset and different castle silhouette are not an exact reference match. The reference has a denser purple dusk city, scattered warm windows and more restrained environmental light. Current generated panel ornaments and reused Ivan portrait also differ in shape and detail. Count lettering has a thin engraved gold appearance, while the reference's booster counts read as fuller cream/gold raised numerals. The reused settings emblem differs from the reference's thicker round metal rim. These are fidelity differences rather than absent resources.

On the tall phone fixture, the board keeps its proportions, with substantially larger scenery gaps above and below it. The previous layout checks establish containment, not an identical composition to the shorter reference.

The small bar below Ivan is **present** and bound to average goal completion; collect goals start at zero. The reference's partially filled bar does not establish its intended data semantics. Copying a percentage would fabricate progress. Similarly, different level numbers, tile arrangements and target/stock/score values are state differences. Brown shields, round medallions, stone/web/bomb and alternate gem families remain available but are not automatically additional playable types; their mapping needs a deliberate gameplay decision. Do not insert them randomly as cosmetic replacements for established identities.

## Recommended focused next pass

First refine the always-visible **board frame/cells, moves medallion, goal frame, score inset and empty stars**. Keep the supplied normal gems/booster artwork and existing rules, input, stock and progress bindings. Build any new frame as an isolated asset or native ornament; use live labels and interactive controls. Update the real Gameplay prefab and owning composition together, then compare actual RU/EN and portrait captures.

Follow with event-driven selection/activation artwork using the supplied state sheet, and a separate tutorial/Pause/toast alignment. Current Gameplay remains pending acceptance. No new revision has been implemented by this audit.
