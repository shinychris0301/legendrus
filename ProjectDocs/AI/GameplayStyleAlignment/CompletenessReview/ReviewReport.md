# Gameplay completeness review — 14 September 2026

**The Gameplay design remains incomplete.** R2 improved selected frames, but did not finish the upper/lower composition or the artwork for targeting, activation, damage and destruction. Four inventory boosters have installed icons and working actions; that does not mean their complete visual designs are finished. No new visual revision or approval is implied by this review.

**Recommended next action**

Finalize a complete Gameplay component/state specification before another small frame-only pass. First define the separate header and footer, readable counts and non-obstructing messages. In the same specification settle the row/column family and show what each of the four existing booster actions should look like when armed, targeted, activated and completed. Then define ice/chain/crate damage and removal states, with stone/web explicitly reserved until their mechanics are specified.

Implement that specification in bounded passes: (1) the full upper/lower composition and messages; (2) the existing booster/special effects and obstacle state art; (3) tutorial/Pause consistency. Reuse the approved normal gems and medallions, preserve live rules/services and update the real prefab and owner together. Validate the action and damage states in Unity as well as the idle page. **These are recommendations, not completed or approved revisions.**
