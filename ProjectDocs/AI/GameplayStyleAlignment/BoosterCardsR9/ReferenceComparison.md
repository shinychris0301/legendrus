# Booster cards R9 — reference comparison

The [verified user reference](../ExtractedGemsReview/Gameplay_Reference_Source.png) shows booster objects over dark wood cards. R8 instead nested each object in a complete blue-backed circular medallion. R9 removes that duplicate rim/backing from the original dedicated booster atlas, exposing the card wood around the exact central mace, firebird, lion shield and bottle.

The objects retain their own metal decoration, source colors, current names, stock and gameplay meaning. Living Light still uses the existing lion-shield illustration and clears a selected color; it does not inherit the different reference shield action. Bogatyr Mace clears a selected row/column, Firebird targets a 3×3 area and Sea King's Pearl adds five moves. Original armed feedback remains separate from the removed permanent frame.

New blank wood/gold cards have protected corner slices and a deeper name well. Four cards retain their existing positions with slightly reduced width, increasing gaps to 12 reference pixels and keeping the rightmost card clear of Settings. Explicit two-line RU/EN breaks preserve the wording; badges sit above the names. Inward lower gold leaves were removed after Unity inspection so the longest Russian label rests entirely on wood. R8 gems, board, header, scenery, mission layout and Settings art remain unchanged.

The original object resolution is small: 241×249 mace, 216×233 firebird, 187×214 shield, 172×216 bottle. R9 displays them within 152×156 without fabricating replacement detail. Fine flame/chain edges remain constrained by the original atlas. Source-native pixels are preserved; the new blank card is generated artwork, not an exact screenshot replica.

Header plaque/portrait/star integration, font consistency and scene composition still differ from the reference. Specials/obstacle states, tiny identity markers and activation effects remain future work. Next focused refinement: the upper HUD materials and spacing, keeping the existing character and live data.
