# R8 reference comparison

The located [941×1672 reference](../ExtractedGemsReview/Gameplay_Reference_Source.png) is the target for this review. Its SHA256 is `a1f5768c4c9b74ee9d631784ae531559c05ea618cace93d13364f79d8fa20852`; the owning Gameplay marker now records it. The source-native extracts are reference inputs only. The larger gray-background chat sheet is a different image and has no verified local pixel identity.

R8 restores broad ruby/violet teardrops with inset fleur-de-lis faces, a cyan/cobalt hexagon with a defined inner face, warm nearly square green glass with its botanical emblem, an embossed gold coin and orange glass with its floral sun. R7's yellow faceted crystal was the clearest material mismatch. The final blue silhouette is about0.73 width/height, close to the native reference's0.74; earlier narrow attempts were rejected.

[Board-size comparison](QA/Source_R7_R8_BoardSize.png) is a diagnostic asset sheet, not a page implementation. [Pixel diagnostics](QA/BoardSizeDiagnostics.json) show rich saturation in the source itself. R8 is not uniformly darker than R7: several pieces gain readable face highlights while the coin loses the old crystal's broad brightness. No blanket desaturation was applied. Bright reflections remain concentrated within the gems; normal sprites have no external halo.

Actual Unity review confirms separated cell edges, distinct gold/orange silhouettes and shared mission icons. An independent reviewer found no blocking alpha, clipping or recognition issue at reference and narrow-phone sizes. Remaining gem differences: blue is the strongest cyan/white accent, gold is smoother, and purple/emblems are more sculpted than the target's painterly finish. Green's bevel remains bright. These are source-guided adaptations, not pixel-exact copies or a proven improvement in prolonged visual comfort.

The current board frame/cell material, scenery composition, upper plaques/font/stars, footer card spacing and circular booster rims still differ from the target. R8 preserves those areas to keep this pass limited to normal gems. Original specials, rainbow idle/activation lighting, small identity markers, obstacle damage art and effects remain follow-up work. Next focused implementation: isolate the existing booster objects from their baked circular rims/backings and finish card label space, retaining names, quantities and actions; then refine the header.
