The medallion lower rims were partly hidden by stock/plus circles in the flattened reference. Donor repairs are approximations, not recovered hidden pixels. Opaque navy interiors are intentional. Fine alpha contours and enlarged source-resolution detail remain imperfect. The 57,635 unchanged visible subject RGB pixels establish color preservation only.
