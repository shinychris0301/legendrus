1. Vision & Solo Development Approach
You are developing this game alone. This Bible is written so one person can build a high-quality, monetizable Match-3 game step by step without a full team.
Core principle: Build the vertical slice first (Chapter 1 fully playable + monetization), then expand. Never try to finish all 20 chapters before launch.
Recommended tech stack for solo:
Unity 2022 LTS or 2023 LTS + Unity UI / UI Toolkit (or Godot 4 if preferred)
Firebase or PlayFab for backend (auth, leaderboards, remote config, analytics)
Unity IAP + AdMob / AppLovin MAX for monetization
Addressables or Asset Bundles for chapter content streaming
Simple custom Match-3 engine (no need for complex third-party board plugins at start)

4. Full Story Bible (Writer)
4.1 Overall Story in One Paragraph
The World Tree is wounded. Living Lights that once fed all of Rus are fading. A young descendant of the bogatyrs discovers the Matching Magic and, guided by a wounded Firebird, travels across twenty realms — from the native village through forests, underwater kingdoms, mountains, mythical Moscow, Baikal, and finally to the roots and crown of the World Tree — to restore the light and balance Chernobog’s darkness. Along the way the hero gains allies (Vasilisa, the three bogatyrs, even a complicated Baba Yaga) and faces classic villains (Koschei, Nightingale the Robber, Zmey Gorynych). The story ends with the rebirth of Rus and an open door for future seasons.
4.2 Story Triggers – When Story Plays
Chapter Start – short intro dialogue + illustration (5–15 seconds of text)
Every 10–15 levels inside a chapter – small character moment or lore line
Major milestone levels (boss or story gate) – longer dialogue
Chapter Complete – full summary + emotional beat + unlock next chapter
Character unlock – dedicated short scene
Do NOT force story after every single level. Players who want pure puzzle can skip most dialogue (always give Skip button).

5. Character Bible
Hero – Customizable (Ivan / Masha default). Grows from ordinary to legend.
Firebird – Main companion, guide, sarcastic but loyal. Visual progress: gains more fire over chapters.
Vasilisa – Equal partner, wise, brave. Romance/friendship option.
Baba Yaga – Most popular. Ambiguous, funny, dangerous, eventually ally. High merch potential.
Ilya Muromets, Dobrynya, Alyosha – Mentors and power-ups in later chapters.
Koschei – Classic multi-phase boss. Death hidden in nested objects (level objective design).
Chernobog – Final villain. Felt as absence of color long before he appears.
For each major character prepare: portrait, full-body, 3–4 emotion variants, short voice line list (even if text-only at launch).
6. Level Design Framework (Level Designer Role)
You cannot hand-craft 200 000 levels. Use a hybrid system:
Story Path: 300–600 carefully designed levels per chapter (hand-authored or heavily curated).
Challenge / Side path: variants with different goals, higher difficulty, collectibles.
Endless / Infinite Realm mode: procedural or semi-procedural boards using chapter theme tiles and rules. High-score + exclusive rewards.
Daily / Event levels: generated or from a large pool, count toward chapter progress.
Level goals types to implement:
Score
Collect N of color X
Clear all blockers (ice, chains, boxes, etc.)
Bring special items down
Survive N moves / timed
Boss damage (match next to boss or special gem)
Nested object (Koschei-style)
Difficulty curve: gentle in Chapter 1, rising peaks before chapter end, recovery after. Always allow continue with soft/hard currency.
7. Monetization Strategy (Full)
7.1 Core Economy
Energy / Lives – regenerates over time (e.g. 1 per 20–30 min, max 5–8). Main gate.
Gold (soft) – from levels, daily, ads. Used for continues, some boosters.
Gems (hard) – mainly from IAP and rare rewards. Used for premium boosters, energy, special packs.
Boosters – consumable. Sold in shop and as level-start upsells.
7.2 Main Revenue Sources
Continue after fail (gold or gem) – highest frequency
Energy refill packs
Booster packs and single boosters
Starter packs and “remove ads” (if hybrid)
Seasonal / event limited offers (highest ARPU)
Character skins / cosmetic frames (emotional spending)
Rewarded video (x2 rewards, free energy, free booster) – carefully balanced
7.3 Key Monetization Moments in Story
Out of moves → continue offer with story flavor (“The light is fading… one more try?”)
Out of energy → “The Living Lights need rest” + timer + offers
Pre-level → “Take the Firebird’s feather with you?”
Chapter end → celebration pack
Character rescue events → strong emotional IAP
7.4 Pricing Guidance (Russia / CIS)
Use local prices. Typical successful range for this genre in RU: small packs 99–299 ₽, medium 499–999 ₽, large / battle pass style 1 499–2 999 ₽. Always show value clearly.

9. Shop, Boosters & Gem Design Specs
9.1 Core Boosters (implement these first)
Перо Жар-Птицы (Firebird Feather) – destroys one gem + neighbors or creates a line blast
Ступа Бабы-Яги (Yaga’s Mortar) – area clear (3×3 or 5×5)
Булава Богатыря (Bogatyr Mace) – destroys entire row or column
Гусли-самогуды – shuffles or converts random gems
Жемчужина Морского Царя – freezes time / adds moves
Живой Огонь (Living Light) – special win condition helper or powerful single clear
Each booster needs: icon, short description, use animation, inventory count.
9.2 Gem Design Direction
6–8 base colors. Each gem should look like a carved Slavic jewel or painted ornament, not generic candy. Soft inner glow. Special gems (striped, wrapped, color bomb equivalents) should feel magical – Firebird feather trail, ice crystal, etc.
Recommended colors: Ruby Red, Sapphire Blue, Emerald Green, Amber Gold, Amethyst Purple, Topaz Orange, optional Diamond White and Dark Obsidian for special levels.
