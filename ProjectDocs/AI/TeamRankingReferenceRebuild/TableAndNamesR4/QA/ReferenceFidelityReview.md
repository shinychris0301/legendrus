# R4 reference fidelity review

Compared native R4 Russian and narrow English captures against the original and R3. The scoped table/name pass is improved, but full-page identity is not claimed.

## Improved

- Dark continuous navy surface replaces brighter grey patterned row boxes; dividers are thin and source-derived.
- The player-guild row has a dark interior with a gold highlight, closer to the original. Its longer sample plaque remains an intentional disclosure.
- Every current RU/EN guild name occupies one line, stays inside its column and preserves the complete text. Long names use smaller sizes within the approved font family.
- R3 reward geometry and clearance remain unchanged. Podium ribbons, crests, title and Details lettering are preserved.

## Remaining differences

- Repaired navy texture repeats; hidden surfaces and a few restored rail spans are approximate.
- The selected live font is wider/heavier than the original. Long names therefore need a smaller size, especially in narrow English. Single-line fit is not exact source-font reproduction.
- The highlighted plaque, tabs, preview/date typography, footer banners/candles and background differ. The source footer banners/candles are not included in this pass.
- Reward enclosure scaling, smoothed ribbon repairs and the approved shared sapphire remain documented R3 differences.
- Shared header/live saved values, sample rankings/rewards and unknown season dates are deliberate product-state adaptations.
